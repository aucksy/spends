Category screen — three ways to fix it

Same screen, same real numbers from your House Maintenance Jpr screenshot. The job of this screen is to answer one question in about two seconds: “is what I spent on this normal for me?” Right now it shows two equally loud numbers and leaves you to do the maths.

What's actually wrong today

  1. Two headline numbers of identical weight. ₹13,814 and ₹15,339 are the same size and boldness, so your eye doesn't know which one is the point.
  2. They cover different spans — six months vs one cycle — and nothing on screen says how they relate.
  3. Two separate controls (3M/6M/All, and the cycle arrows) sit near each other but drive different numbers. Easy to assume one drives both.
  4. “SPENT IN THIS CYCLE ONLY” — the word “only” is doing defensive work. It's compensating for a layout that's ambiguous, instead of the layout being clear.
  5. The dates over the list aren't obeyed by the list. Your 23 Jul row sits under a “25 Jul – 24 Aug” heading. That's correct — in Smart Cycle, Spends buckets by the card's billing day — but nothing says so, so it reads as a bug.

Why this works

  • Fixes all five problems.
  • One number is obviously the point; the other is context.
  • The comparison is written out, so no mental arithmetic.
  • The 3M/6M control sits inside the comparison box — it's obvious it only changes “usual”.
  • The 23 Jul row explains itself instead of looking wrong.

Why this works

  • Best for spotting a trend, not just this month.

  • You'd have seen “Jun was worse” at a glance.
  • Most work to build, and the chart needs enough history to look right.
My recommendation: Option A. It's the only one that answers the question in words rather than leaving you to compare two numbers, and it fixes every one of the five problems. Option B is the safe pick if you'd rather keep the screen familiar — it's mostly a re-arrangement of what's already there. Option C is the nicest to look at and the most useful over time, but it's the biggest build and it needs six months of history before it looks like anything.

All three drop “SPENT IN THIS CYCLE ONLY”, rename “Monthly average” to “Usual month”, put each control inside the number it changes, and tag the row that's billed into this cycle from an earlier date.
