Spends — Privacy Policy

Effective 4 July 2026 · Last updated 3 August 2026

Spends is an offline-first personal expense tracker for Android. It has no account, no ads, and no analytics or tracking on your financial data. Your information stays on your device — and, only if you choose to turn it on, in your own Google Drive for backup. Nothing is sent to any other company. We (the developer) never receive, store, or have access to your financial data or your messages.

In one line: Spends reads your bank's transaction SMS on your device only to pre-fill an expense for you to review and save — nothing is ever added without your confirmation. Nothing is ever uploaded to us, and nothing is sent to any other company. SMS access is optional — the app works fully with manual entry if you don't grant it. The only connection Spends ever makes is to your own Google Drive, and only if you switch backup on.

1. Information Spends accesses — and why

SMS messages (optional)

With your explicit permission, Spends reads incoming and existing SMS messages on your device to detect bank and credit-card transaction alerts and pre-fill an expense for you to confirm. This parsing happens entirely on your phone. The content of your messages is never transmitted to us, and never to anyone else. Spends does not use SMS data for advertising or marketing. You can use the entire app without granting SMS access (via manual entry), and you can revoke the permission at any time in Android Settings.

Transactions and financial information you enter or capture

The expenses, income, categories, accounts/cards, and notes you add — whether typed manually or captured from an SMS — are stored in a private database on your device.

Notifications

With your permission, Spends posts a notification when it detects a transaction, so you can review it and save it, and to deliver your daily recurring-entry reminder.

2. Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to enable backup, Spends stores a copy of your app data in your own Google Drive, in a "Spends Backup" folder that the app creates. Spends uses the restricted drive.file scope, which means it can only ever access the files it created itself — it can never see the rest of your Drive. This backup belongs to you and lives in your Google account — the developer cannot see or access it. Data is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. You can delete the backup at any time from within Spends or from your Google Drive. Backup is off by default.

3. Data we collect and share

We do not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal data, financial data, or messages with anyone, and nothing is sent to the developer at all, ever. There is no server or account behind Spends, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Spends makes network connections in only two situations, both of which you switch on yourself:

  • Google Drive backup — to your own Drive, and only if you enable backup.
  • AI currency conversion — see the callout below.

3a. AI currency conversion (optional, off by default)

What it does. If you keep your books in one currency and a bank alerts you in another, Spends can ask an AI provider what the exchange rate is, so the transaction is recorded in your own currency instead of being logged as the wrong number. Every converted transaction shows you the original amount and the rate that was used.

You must turn it on and supply your own API key (Anthropic, OpenAI or Groq — your choice). Until you do both, Spends makes no AI network call of any kind.

What is sent. Only a currency question — for example "How many INR is 1 MYR right now?". No transaction amount, no merchant, no account or card number, and no message text is ever sent. The provider is asked for an exchange rate and nothing else; the arithmetic happens on your phone. Rates are reused for six hours, so a day's alerts from one bank typically cost a single request.

Your key. It is encrypted on your device, is never included in a backup, is never shown back to you once saved, and is only ever sent to the provider you selected. You can remove it at any time in Settings → Currency & AI. Your use of that provider is governed by their terms and privacy policy.

Avoiding it entirely. You can pin your own exchange rate in the same settings screen, which Spends uses instead — no key, and no network call.

A different, unrelated "AI helper" existed between v1.56.0 and v1.64.0 and could send number-masked transaction information to a provider. It was removed in v1.65.0, is not part of the feature described above, and the key it used is erased the next time you open Spends.

4. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you remove it. You can:

  • Delete individual entries in the app (with a Trash/undo step), or empty the Trash to remove them permanently;
  • Clear the app's data from Android Settings, or uninstall Spends, to remove all on-device data;
  • Delete your Google Drive backup from within Spends or directly in Google Drive.

6. Children

Spends is a general-purpose finance tool and is not directed to children under 13.

7. Security

Your data is stored in your device's private app storage. Backups, if enabled, are transferred to your Google Drive over an encrypted HTTPS connection. If you enable AI currency conversion, the exchange-rate question it asks is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to the provider you chose, and carries no transaction data. No financial data or message content is transmitted to the developer.
