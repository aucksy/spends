Spends — Privacy Policy

Effective 4 July 2026 · Last updated 26 July 2026

Spends is an offline-first personal expense tracker for Android. It has no account, no ads, and no analytics or tracking on your financial data. Your information stays on your device — and, only if you choose to turn them on, in your own Google Drive (backup) and with a third-party AI provider in the limited form described in section 3. We (the developer) never receive, store, or have access to your financial data or your messages.

In one line: Spends reads your bank's transaction SMS on your device only to help you log expenses in one tap. Nothing is ever uploaded to us. SMS access is optional — the app works fully with manual entry if you don't grant it. One feature can send limited data to a third party: the optional AI helper, which is off by default and needs your own API key — see section 3.

1. Information Spends accesses — and why

SMS messages (optional)

With your explicit permission, Spends reads incoming and existing SMS messages on your device to detect bank and credit-card transaction alerts and pre-fill an expense for you to confirm. This parsing happens entirely on your phone. The content of your messages is never transmitted to us, and never to any third party unless you switch on the optional AI helper described in section 3 — which is off by default and sends only a number-masked extract. Spends does not use SMS data for advertising or marketing. You can use the entire app without granting SMS access (via manual entry), and you can revoke the permission at any time in Android Settings.

Transactions and financial information you enter or capture

The expenses, income, categories, accounts/cards, and notes you add — whether typed manually or captured from an SMS — are stored in a private database on your device.

Notifications

With your permission, Spends posts a notification when it detects a transaction so you can add it in one tap, and to deliver your daily recurring-entry reminder.

2. Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to enable backup, Spends stores a copy of your app data in your own Google Drive, in a "Spends Backup" folder that the app creates. Spends uses the restricted drive.file scope, which means it can only ever access the files it created itself — it can never see the rest of your Drive. This backup belongs to you and lives in your Google account — the developer cannot see or access it. Data is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. You can delete the backup at any time from within Spends or from your Google Drive. Backup is off by default.

3. Optional AI helper (off by default, your own key)

Spends includes an optional "AI helper" that can suggest a category for a detected transaction and write a short plain-English summary of a spending cycle. It is off by default and does nothing until you both switch it on and paste your own API key, which is stored encrypted on your device and is never included in a backup. While it is on — and only for the sub-feature you enable — the following is sent over HTTPS to Groq, a third-party AI provider:

  • Category suggestions: the merchant name as your bank wrote it; whether the transaction was money in or out; the words of that one bank SMS or notification with every number replaced by a single "#"; your category names; and the names of merchants you previously categorised.
  • Spending insights: your category totals and income/expense totals for the cycle you are viewing.

The masking replaces each whole number with one "#", so amounts, balances, account and card numbers, numeric dates and phone numbers are removed outright — and because the digit count goes too, not even the size of an amount is visible ("Rs.5,59,393.44" becomes "Rs.#"). Being straight with you about its limits: it removes numbers, not letters. So a payee's name written in the message (for example "to JOHN DOE"), a month written as text ("Jun"), the letters of a mixed reference ("AB12CD" becomes "AB#CD"), and a merchant name that itself contains digits are all sent as-is.

Never sent for either sub-feature: your full unmasked message text, any amount or balance, your account or card numbers, or your individual transaction records. The AI helper can never add, edit or delete a transaction — it only proposes a category that you still confirm yourself. Switching the helper off stops all of this immediately. What you send is then handled under Groq's own privacy policy.

4. Data we collect and share

We do not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal data, financial data, or messages — nothing is sent to the developer at all, and there is no server or account behind Spends. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. Apart from the optional AI helper described in section 3, the only network connection the app makes is to your own Google Drive, and only if you enable backup.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you remove it. You can:

  • Delete individual entries in the app (with a Trash/undo step), or empty the Trash to remove them permanently;
  • Clear the app's data from Android Settings, or uninstall Spends, to remove all on-device data;
  • Delete your Google Drive backup from within Spends or directly in Google Drive.

7. Children

Spends is a general-purpose finance tool and is not directed to children under 13.

8. Security

Your data is stored in your device's private app storage. Backups, if enabled, are transferred to your Google Drive over an encrypted HTTPS connection. No financial data or message content is transmitted to the developer.
