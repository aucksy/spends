Version: 1.75.1
• Spends can now find your bank accounts and credit cards in your SMS. It asks you first. Say yes, and it looks at the last 1 year of bank SMS on this phone only. Say no, and you can add them yourself.
• For each bank or card it finds, Spends asks you once to check it and add it. For a card, set its bill date. Then the card's spends count in the same month as your card bill.
• You can see and add them any time in Settings → Money & Cycles → Banks & Cards.
