Version: 1.76.0
• Your months now follow your salary. If your salary comes a few days early or late, that month starts on the day it really came.
• New setup question: "When did your last salary come?" Pick the day, or say it has not come yet. Spends then spots your salary and sets the day for you.
• No monthly salary? Choose "I don't get a monthly salary" and Spends uses calendar months.
• To turn this off, go to Settings → Money & Cycles → "Follow my salary date".
